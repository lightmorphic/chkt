Chkt rings, then speaks your reminder out loud, so you know what needs
doing without touching your phone.

Features:

* Spoken reminders through your phone's text-to-speech engine (SherpaTTS
  recommended, free and offline). Per-reminder alert styles: ringtone +
  speech, ringtone only, speech only, or a plain notification, with an
  optional tone before the speech.
* Exact alarms that survive reboots and battery savers, bypass Do Not
  Disturb, and show a full-screen alert like an alarm clock.
* Multiple reminder lists.
* Flexible repeats: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, custom intervals.
* Snooze from 10 minutes up to a day.
* Tap-to-record widget: tap, say "remind me at 2pm to feed the cat", done.
  Parsed on your device, audio never leaves your phone.
* Location reminders when you arrive at or leave a place, using Android's
  own location services.
* Quiet hours, a do-not-disturb schedule for Chkt itself.
* Daily backups, plus export/import as plain JSON or markdown.
* Optional sync with a self-hosted Chkt Server (off by default, the app
  is fully usable offline, forever).
* Lightweight completion statistics.

No ads, no tracking, no Google services, no account. GPL-3.0.
